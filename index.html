How this works

One card per thing on the drive: a movie, a game, a folder of documents. The local AI (running on this computer, nothing leaves it) suggests where each one belongs. You decide. Nothing is copied or deleted while you swipe; you review the plan under 🚚 Apply afterwards.

✅

Swipe right → AI pick

Agree with the green box on the card: it goes where the AI suggests, e.g. a movie to the Jellyfin movies folder as “Inception (2010)”.

🎯

Swipe up ↑ Choose

The AI is wrong, or you want another name. Pick the place yourself (Movies, Shows, Games, Music, Trading, Photos, Documents, Software, Archive) and optionally type the folder name.

📦

Swipe down ↓ Archive

Keep it, but don't sort it. It's copied as-is to the configured archive destination with the same folder path. The safe choice when unsure.

🗑

Swipe left ← Delete

Not worth keeping. On a read-only source, the item is skipped; the source remains unchanged. On a normal drive it goes to a _fileswipe_trash folder first, never straight to deletion.

📂 Split up: a folder holds several different things (e.g. a “Movies” folder)? Split it, and each thing inside gets its own card.
⤴ Whole parent: the opposite. Too many tiny cards from one folder? Decide the folder as one card.
📚 Whole folder…: send every card in this folder to one place in one tap (e.g. all of Games → AI pick).
Undo: the ↩ Undo button, or Z; it works again and again, also for split/merge and whole-folder moves.
Changed your mind? Use the Keep / Delete tabs at the top, tap an item, and decide again.
Keyboard: → AI pick · ↑ choose · ↓ archive · ← delete · S split · L later · ? help
AI still thinking? The box says “first guess” until the local model has looked at that card. That takes about 20 seconds per card in the background; you can swipe meanwhile.
